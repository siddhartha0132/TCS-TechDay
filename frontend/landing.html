05 · Questions the jury may ask

FAQ

Does the AI decide who gets a loan?

No. The AI only understands what you say and explains the result. The yes/no, the EMI and the maximum amount come from a deterministic rules engine whose policy lives in one JSON file — the same input always gives the same, auditable answer.

How accurate is it, and how did you measure it?

In rules mode, 36 of 36 labelled multilingual conversations reach the expected verdict, and an invariant fuzzer over 30,000 random profiles found zero rule errors (EMI and FOIR maths, monotonicity, and every “you qualify for up to X” and improvement tip re-verified). Honest limits: the labelled set is small and written by the same team, and AI-mode accuracy on blind messages from other people is the next thing to measure.

Could a misread number give a wrong verdict?

That's why there's a confirmation step: before any verdict, the bot lists the exact values it will use and flags unusual ones (e.g. “monthly income ₹6.5 L — is that per year?”). Nothing is decided until the applicant confirms, and any correction asks again.

What if the AI service is down or slow?

Replies have a hard 13-second budget. Models are tried in a chain (several free models from two providers); if all are busy, the built-in rules agent answers. Decisions never depend on an external API being up.

Does checking affect my credit score?

No. It is an indicative soft check: no credit bureau enquiry is made. Final approval is always subject to document verification.

What about privacy?

PAN, Aadhaar, phone, email, card and account numbers are masked before anything else sees the text — with checksum logic so real rupee amounts aren't destroyed. Sessions live in memory only and expire; nothing is written to disk.

Is it secure enough for a bank?

We red-teamed it: rate limits, request-size limits, strict input validation, security headers and CSP, unguessable sessions, no internal error leakage. The attack suite ships with the code and re-runs in one command.

Which loans and languages are supported?

Personal, home, car and education loans. English, हिंदी, Hinglish, বাংলা, தமிழ், తెలుగు, मराठी, ગુજરાતી, ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ and ଓଡ଼ିଆ — by text or voice.

Different banks have different rates — does it handle that?

Yes. The same applicant is checked against a panel of four lenders (public bank, private bank, small finance bank, NBFC), each with its own rate, CIBIL cut-off and FOIR limit, and ranked by who can lend the full amount at the lowest rate. The panel is simulated with fictional names; a real deployment would plug in each lender's eligibility API.

Is this real customer data?

No. All applicants are synthetic, and the bank policy is an illustrative synthetic policy, as the problem statement recommends.

How would a bank take this to production?

Swap the policy JSON for the bank's rules, plug in Account Aggregator for verified income, KYC and bureau APIs behind the same tools, move sessions to Redis, and add WhatsApp as a channel. The Docker image is ready.
